Privacy Policy for LightClimber

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Developer: Failed Studio

1. Overview

Failed Studio ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use LightClimber on the Meta Horizon Store.

2. Information Collection and Storage (Local-Only)

We are committed to data privacy. LightClimber does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

All game state and preference information is saved exclusively on your local device storage and never leaves your headset. Specifically, the game writes only the following data locally:

  • Music Settings: Your preferred audio or volume configurations.
  • Outfit Selection: Your customized character appearance choices.
  • Progress Stage: Your current level, checkpoint, or game progression milestones.

3. Third-Party Services

Because all data remains locally on your device, we do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third-party analytics tools, ad networks, or external servers.

4. Data Access and Deletion

Since all save data resides entirely on your local hardware:

  • You have full control over your data at all times.
  • You can view, modify, or completely delete your saved settings, outfit choices, and progress stage at any time by clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling LightClimber from your headset.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our external support destination or contact Failed Studio directly.
